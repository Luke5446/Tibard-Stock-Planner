Once it is in, every container plan is checked against the metres Clockwork hold, and the planner's Fabric column shows where the cloth runs short.

In stock and On PO are Clockwork's metres. Our demand = metres the open (unshipped) containers and the cartons being planned will cut. Free = in stock − our demand; After PO = free + on PO. Critical short, and no PO lands within 2 weeks to cover it. Low short but a PO lands within 2 weeks, or free is under a month of Clockwork's average usage. Click a row for the ETAs and the container-by-container cover; click a heading to sort.

🔗 Codes and their cloth

A code's cloth comes from Clockwork's own style list on the report, its colour from our Sage description, and its metres per garment from the production planner's costing figures (a customer variant without one takes its base style's). Anything that cannot be worked out is listed here: pick the cloth, type the metres, press Save, and every size of that style follows. The planner's Fabric column shows ? for a code with no cloth.
